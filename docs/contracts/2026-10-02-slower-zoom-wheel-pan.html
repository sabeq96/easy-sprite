Slower zoom and editor wheel-pan (#16, first iteration)

agreed2026-10-02approved 2026-10-02docs/contracts/2026-10-02-slower-zoom-wheel-pan.html
Problem
Every wheel event moves a whole zoom-ladder step, so one trackpad pinch flies through the whole range F1 F2; the pixel editor has no wheel/two-finger scrolling at all F1.
Goal
Pinch and ⌘/ctrl+wheel zoom one ladder step per 100 px of accumulated delta in both editors, and a plain wheel / two-finger scroll pans the pixel editor.

What gets built

newchangedremoveduntouched

Zoom gestures no longer jump a ladder rung per event: their scroll amount is added up, and every 100 px of it moves one rung (same rungs as the + and − buttons), anchored at the cursor in the pixel editor as today. In the pixel editor a plain mouse wheel or two-finger scroll now moves the sprite around instead of zooming; ⌘/ctrl+wheel and pinch zoom. The sheet builder keeps its native scrolling and layout; only its ⌘/pinch zoom slows down.
